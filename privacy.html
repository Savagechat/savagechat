Summary

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Messages you type are sent securely to OpenAI to generate AI responses.
  • We do not store payment information.
  • You must agree before messages are sent for AI processing.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to Savage Chat (the “App”). If you have questions, contact us at savagechat@mail.com.

Information we collect

1) Chat Content

  • The text you type into the App.
  • Your selected mood preference (e.g., Savage, Happy, Ultra).

Chat messages are transmitted securely to OpenAI to generate AI responses.

Savage Chat does not require account registration and does not collect your name, email address, or direct personal identifiers unless you voluntarily contact support.

Third-Party AI Processing (OpenAI)

Savage Chat uses OpenAI to generate AI responses.

Data sent to OpenAI includes:

  • The message text you enter
  • Your selected mood setting

Messages are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

OpenAI processes this data solely to generate responses. OpenAI’s data handling practices are governed by their Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/privacy.

Savage Chat does not sell or profile users and does not use chat content for advertising.

Purchases and subscriptions

  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple via In-App Purchase.
  • We receive subscription status only (e.g., whether Ultra is active).
  • We do not receive or store payment card details.

Advertising

The app may display rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob. Ad providers may collect device identifiers or usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

How we use your information

  • To generate AI responses using OpenAI.
  • To verify subscription access.
  • To improve app stability and performance.
  • To respond to support requests.

User Consent

Before chat messages are sent to OpenAI, users are presented with a clear consent screen explaining that messages will be processed by OpenAI. Users must agree before using AI features.

Data retention

Savage Chat does not permanently store chat conversations on our servers. Messages are processed in real time to generate responses.

Security

We use industry-standard encryption and safeguards to protect transmitted information.

Children

Savage Chat is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

International transfers

Data sent to OpenAI may be processed in countries outside your own. Appropriate safeguards are applied to protect information.
